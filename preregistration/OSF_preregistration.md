## 2. Research question

Under what conditions does routine cognitive offloading to AI raise a learner's
measured performance while lowering their unassisted capability?

## 3. Hypotheses and decision rules

**H1 (bistability).** Above kappa*, the model admits two stable equilibria
separated by a threshold.
*Status: already established analytically (Prop. 3, 4). Not an empirical claim.*

**H2 (divergence).** A non-empty region of parameter space exists where
observed performance P rises while capability S falls.
*Status: already established (Prop. 5). Not an empirical claim.*

**H3 (chess, fitting domain).** Fitting the model to chess player-year
trajectories across the engine era will yield r_cap < 0.2 and will not place
the population in the trap basin. Chess bans engines in rated play, so the
model predicts no capability decline despite decades of engine use in
preparation.
*Falsified if:* the fitted chess population sits in the trap basin, or absolute
skill (mean centipawn loss) declines across the engine era.

**H4 (education, PRIMARY TEST - blind).** Studies of generative AI in education
that measure outcomes with AI REMOVED (delayed post-test, no tool access) will
report smaller effect sizes than studies measuring outcomes with AI PRESENT.

Prediction registered now, before any literature coding:
- direction: d(AI-removed) < d(AI-present)
- the model, with chess-fitted parameters and only r_cap and g changed per
  config/model_default.yaml, generates a predicted difference; the point
  estimate and 90% interval will be computed by posterior predictive
  simulation in scripts/07 BEFORE the literature coding in scripts/08.
- *Confirmed if:* the observed pooled difference falls inside the predicted
  interval and has the predicted sign.
- *Falsified if:* d(AI-removed) >= d(AI-present), or the observed difference
  falls outside the predicted interval.

**This is the strongest test in the project.** The AI-removed subset of the
education literature has never been separately meta-analysed. The analyst has
not seen this pooled value and cannot anticipate it.

**H5 (colonoscopy, secondary test - held out, NOT blind).** The same fitted
parameters, mapped to the colonoscopy setting, will predict a decline in
unassisted detection performance of the order reported by Budzyn et al. (2025).

## 4. Declared limitation on H5

The colonoscopy result (Budzyn et al. 2025, Lancet Gastroenterol Hepatol
10(10):896-903, DOI 10.1016/S2468-1253(25)00133-5; ADR fell 28.4% to 22.4%) is
**already published and known to the analyst.** H5 is therefore a held-out
test, not a blind one. Mitigations, locked here in advance:

1. The parameter mapping from chess to colonoscopy is fixed in
   config/model_default.yaml as of this registration and may not be changed.
2. A prediction INTERVAL is generated by a specified procedure (posterior
   predictive), not chosen by hand.
3. No parameter may be adjusted after the H5 comparison is computed.
4. This limitation appears in the paper's Methods section, not a footnote.

H5 is reported as corroboration only. The paper's empirical weight rests on H4.

## 5. Analysis plan

1. **Fit (scripts/07).** Chess player-year panel. Maximum likelihood first,
   then Bayesian posterior with weakly informative priors. Absolute skill
   measured by mean centipawn loss against a fixed engine at fixed depth, not
   Elo (which is relative and inflates).
2. **Identifiability.** Profile likelihoods and posterior pair plots before any
   held-out prediction. If beta and c are not separately identified, they will
   be combined into the single loop-gain parameter beta*c and reported as such.
3. **Predict (scripts/08).** Held-out predictions computed once. No iteration.
4. **Sensitivity.** Global sensitivity analysis over all parameters; results
   reported whether or not they favour the hypotheses.

## 6. Sample and inclusion criteria (H4)

Studies eligible for the education split:
- empirical, generative-AI intervention, learner outcome measured quantitatively
- published 2022-2026
- reports enough to compute a standardised effect size
- outcome classifiable as AI-PRESENT (tool available at measurement) or
  AI-REMOVED (tool withheld at measurement)
- studies where classification is ambiguous are coded as such and excluded from
  the primary comparison, with a sensitivity analysis including them

Coding will be done by the analyst; a random 20% subsample will be independently
coded by a second person to estimate agreement (Cohen's kappa reported).

## 7. Kill criteria

- If H3 fails, the fitting domain is invalid and the project restarts with a
  different fitting domain.
- If the model cannot be identified from chess data, it is simplified to the
  reduced one-dimensional form and the paper's claims are narrowed accordingly.
- If H4 is falsified, **this is reported as the headline result.** A falsified
  divergence hypothesis is publishable and honest; a quietly abandoned one is
  not.

   The intercept is now recomputed against the corrected slope. Affects only
   the parameter fit, not the H3 result. Corrected values: S_eq = 0.648,
   delta/g = 0.516.

3. **2026-08-18** — H4 scope reduced. The preregistered systematic review of the
   GenAI-in-education literature was not completed within the project timeline.
   In its place we report a single-study worked example (Bastani et al. 2025,
   PNAS), which reports both an AI-present and an AI-removed outcome for the
   same students. This is a demonstration that the predicted divergence exists,
   NOT a test of its generality. The full review remains future work and the
   paper states this in the Limitations section.

4. **2026-08-18** — H3 test statistic changed from a difference test to a TOST
   equivalence test (SESOI = 3cp). Reason: Proposition 3 predicts the ABSENCE of
   decline, which is an equivalence claim; a significant difference test was
   never the appropriate evidence. The difference test is reported alongside,
   including the two specifications in which it weakened. The equivalence test
   holds in all six specifications. This change was made after seeing the
   robustness output and is disclosed as such.
